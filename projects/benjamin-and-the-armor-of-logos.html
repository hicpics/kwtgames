Benjamin and The Armor of Logos

Technical Level Designer, QA · Action RPG

Benjamin and the Armor of Logos is a mobile action RPG with on-rails, Infinity Blade–style combat. I joined late in development as a Technical Level Designer to clean up the spline pathing for the player character and support QA. I polished the movement along the spline so traversal felt smooth and immersive, and logged bugs into Jira so the team could quickly fix issues before release.
