Lucas Mission Starhope

Combat Designer, QA · Rail Shooter

Lucas Mission Starhope is a Star Fox–style on-rails shooter. I joined late in development to redesign the bosses, since the original encounters all felt too similar. I created new mechanics, attack patterns, and pacing ideas to give each boss its own identity. These redesigned encounters were planned for a later content update, but didn’t make it into release due to layoffs.
