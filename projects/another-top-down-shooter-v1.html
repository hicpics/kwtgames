Another Top Down Shooter V1

University prototype

For the most part this is a quickly thrown together prototype. One thing you may notice is that the enemies drop guns they don't use. Eventually in the final game we were going to have shooting enemies and they would drop guns, and regular melee enemies would drop things like health and shields. Unfortunately we never got that far since we merged with another team and we were forced to start from scratch.

I've cleaned up as many of the bugs that I can find and should be fully playable.
