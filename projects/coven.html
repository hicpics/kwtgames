Coven

Technical Designer · Action RPG

Project Overview

Coven is an action RPG with “Zelda-light” combat that is focused on fast ability use, clear reads, and expressive elemental attacks. I joined the team as a Technical Designer to build the game’s magical abilities from scratch using Unreal’s Gameplay Ability System (GAS), C++, Blueprints, Niagara, and FMOD.

My work covered design, implementation, tuning, VFX, and audio for the core combat abilities that defined the game’s moment-to-moment fighting style.

Ability Design & Implementation

I created a suite of elemental abilities designed to serve distinct gameplay roles and support clear combat readability in a Zelda-style loop. These abilities included:

  • Homing Fireball – target-tracking projectile
  • Fire Tornado – AoE damage-over-time zone
  • Water Shields & Buffs – defensive/offensive stat modifiers
  • Earth Poison & Vine Roots – DOT + movement slow effects
  • Ice Beam – sustained ice beam with timing-based damage

I implemented abilities using:

  • C++ (foundations, logic, custom needs)
  • Blueprints (behavior and scripting)
  • GAS for activation, cooldowns, cost, and attribute interaction
  • Gameplay Effects and Attribute Sets for damage, statuses, and scaling

Each ability required designing its purpose, building behavior, syncing timing with animation, and integrating both VFX and audio cues.

Tuning & Combat Feel

To make abilities feel responsive and readable, I tuned:

  • Damage values and scaling
  • Cooldowns and cast timings
  • Homing behavior
  • Ranges and projectile speeds
  • Status effect durations (DOT, slow, buffs)
  • Telegraph timing/sync with animation

I tested abilities myself and through internal playtests, refining them until they fit the combat rhythm: quick reads, clear intent, and satisfying feedback.

VFX Implementation (Niagara)

I learned Niagara rapidly and created real-time VFX for:

  • Projectile trails and impact bursts
  • Elemental AoE shapes (tornado, poison, vines)
  • Area telegraphs
  • Shield loops and hit flashes
  • Beam effects for ice attacks
  • GPU/CPU emitters depending on performance needs

Readability was a priority. Abilities needed to be understood instantly from an action-RPG camera distance, with elemental identity and clear timing.

Audio Implementation (FMOD + Unreal)

I handled all technical audio setup for abilities using:

  • FMOD (events, parameters, blending)
  • Trigger logic inside ability activation
  • Layered audio (cast → travel → impact)
  • Looping transitions for sustained abilities
  • Distance- and pitch-based modulation
  • Timing sync with VFX and animation

This was my first major project using FMOD, and I learned it quickly to support production needs.

Collaboration

I worked closely with:

  • Animation – syncing timing windows, cast frames, and impacts
  • Engineering – refining GAS behavior and solving technical blockers
  • Design Lead – shaping gameplay roles and combat feel
  • Audio Team/Composer – ensuring FMOD events matched design intent

Typical workflow: prototype → review → sync with animation → refine timing → add VFX → integrate audio → playtest → tune.

Production Constraints

The production environment required fast problem-solving:

  • Tight deadlines
  • Limited VFX assets early on
  • Needed to learn GAS, Niagara, and FMOD extremely quickly

Despite constraints, I delivered a complete and polished elemental ability set.

Impact

My contributions helped define:

  • The game’s core elemental combat identity
  • Ability feel, responsiveness, and clarity
  • Stronger feedback through synced audio and VFX
  • A reliable, extendable ability framework for the team
  • Improved internal impressions of combat pacing and readability

Team feedback specifically highlighted:

  • The homing fireball for its clean tracking and feel
  • The ice tornado/shield for its strong visual impact and clarity

What I’m Proud Of

  • Building full abilities from scratch, not tweaking existing systems
  • Rapidly mastering Niagara, FMOD, and GAS under pressure
  • Creating synced, expressive ability experiences
  • The homing fireball and ice tornado/shield as standout pieces

What I Learned

  • Designing distinct ability roles for action-RPG combat
  • Building C++ → GAS → Blueprint ability architecture
  • Creating readable VFX for a top-down/action camera
  • Implementing and tuning FMOD audio events
  • Aligning gameplay, animation, VFX, and sound into cohesive combat moments
  • Iterative tuning through rapid internal playtesting
