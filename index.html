Technical Game Designer | Combat, Enemy & Encounter Design | UE5, Unity

I’m a Technical Game Designer with 7.5 years of experience and 11 shipped PC and mobile games. I specialize in combat, enemy and boss, and level design, and I work in Unreal Engine 4/5 (Blueprints, C++, Gameplay Ability System) and Unity (C#).

I’m known for fast turnarounds: I overhauled 9 levels in 6 weeks and raised their user test scores from 1–3 to 4.5–5.
